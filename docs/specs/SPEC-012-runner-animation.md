# SPEC-012 — Runner animation

- Status: in-progress
- Milestone: M2 (presentation side-track, opened by the human on 2026-10-26; touches no simulation behavior)
- Depends on: [SPEC-001](SPEC-001-locomotion.md), [SPEC-002](SPEC-002-jump.md), [SPEC-003](SPEC-003-dive.md), [ADR-0001](../decisions/ADR-0001-simulation-presentation-split.md), [ADR-0008](../decisions/ADR-0008-no-knockdown-mario-dive.md)

## Fantasy

The runner's body tells the truth about the motor. Feet match the ground speed, the jump pose follows the real arc, the dive looks committed, and nothing in the animation lags or overrides what the stick just did.

## In scope

- `SK_Runner`: the rigged character mesh, humanoid avatar, swapped into the `Visual` child of `Runner.prefab`.
- `RunnerAnimator` (Presentation): reads the motor and its body each frame, listens to `IMotorEvents`, and drives Animator parameters plus two procedural layers: turn lean and landing squash.
- `RunnerAnimationProfile` (Presentation): the animation feel numbers.
- `AC_Runner`: an Animator Controller generated by an editor tool from clips named by convention.
- Import rules for the character and for clip files (Mixamo or Blender), enforced by an asset postprocessor.

## Out of scope

- Any change to `PlayerMotor`, `MovementProfile`, the capsule, or simulation timing.
- Root motion. Animation never moves the body.
- Facial animation (the mesh ships viseme and blink blend shapes; they stay at zero).
- Grab poses (SPEC-005), VFX, audio, IK foot placement.

## Rules

1. Animation is presentation only. `RunnerAnimator` may read `PlayerMotor.State`, `PlayerMotor.Grounded`, and the rigidbody's velocity and transform. It never writes velocity, rotation, state, or constraints.
2. The Animator has **Apply Root Motion** off, **Update Mode** Normal (it follows the interpolated rigidbody every rendered frame), and **Culling Mode** Always Animate.
3. No transition in `AC_Runner` uses exit time. Every transition is driven by a parameter and uses the profile's `stateCrossfade` as a fixed duration.
4. **Locomotion** is a 1D blend tree on horizontal speed: Idle at 0, Walk at `walkCycleSpeed`, Run at `runCycleSpeed`. Those two values are the ground speeds at which each clip's feet stop sliding; they are calibrated per clip, not tuned for feel. The speed parameter is damped by `locomotionBlendDamp` so the 0.4 s wind-up reads as a wind-up.
5. **Airborne** is a 1D blend tree on vertical speed, not on clip time: Rise above `+airBlendSpeedBand`, Apex at 0, Fall below `−airBlendSpeedBand`. Any jump height, a tap or a full hold, plays the right pose at the right moment because the pose follows the arc.
6. The Animator enters Airborne at once on `Jumped`. Walking off a lip enters Airborne only after `airPoseDelay` without ground, so a one-tick probe flicker does not pop the pose.
7. **Dive** plays while `State == Diving`. The motor already pitches the whole body into the belly-dive, so the Dive clip is authored in body space (arms forward, legs back) with no extra hip pitch.
8. **GetUp** plays while `State == GettingUp`, at a playback speed that makes the clip last exactly `getUpDuration` from `MovementProfile`.
9. **Turn lean** rolls the `Visual` transform into the turn, scaled by yaw rate and horizontal speed, up to `maxTurnLean`, smoothed by `turnLeanResponsiveness`. Lean is zero outside Upright.
10. **Landing squash** on `Landed` scales `Visual` down on Y and out on X/Z by up to `landSquash`, proportional to impact speed up to `landSquashFullImpact`, and recovers over `landSquashDuration`.
11. Clip files live in `Assets/_Project/Art/Runner/Animation/` and are named `A_Runner_<Clip>.fbx`, with `<Clip>` one of `Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `JumpRise`, `JumpApex`, `JumpFall`, `Dive`, `GetUp`. On first import they become humanoid clips. Root rotation (Body Orientation) and height are baked into the pose; horizontal root travel is not, so it is discarded and the mesh never drifts off the capsule. Idle, Walk, Run, JumpApex, JumpFall and Dive loop.
12. A missing clip leaves its state empty, so the controller can be generated before every clip exists. Running **Tumble → Build Runner Animator** again picks up new clips. The builder keeps the old capsule `Body` and `Visor` in the prefab, switched off.

## Acceptance

Run in `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Gym.unity`.

- [ ] `SK_Runner` stands 1.8 m tall inside the capsule gizmo, faces the run direction, and its avatar is valid with no import colliders.
- [ ] From a standstill to full run, the feet do not visibly slide at walk or at full speed.
- [ ] A tap jump and a full-hold jump both show rise, apex, and fall at the right moments, and the runner lands into the run with no hitch or delay.
- [ ] Walking off a lip shows the fall pose; running over the Gym's small steps does not flicker into it.
- [ ] The dive pose reads as committed, and the get-up finishes exactly as control returns.
- [ ] A sharp 90° turn at full speed shows a visible lean; a hard landing shows a squash; neither changes the motor's path (compare to the old capsule visual).

## Authored clips

`A_Runner_Dive` (25 frames at 24 fps, loops with a small flutter) and `A_Runner_GetUp` (13 frames, 0.54 s) are hand-posed on `SK_Runner` in Blender, source `Models/pim-smiling-friends/source/SK_Runner_Animations.blend`. The motor pitches the whole body (`divePitch`) and rights it over `getUpDuration`, so both clips pose the limbs only and never rotate the root.

- Dive: arms swept out and forward in a V, because the head is too big for them to pass straight overhead. Legs trail with a slight flutter.
- GetUp: starts on the Dive pose, tucks the knees and plants the hands (a push-up), springs with the arms out for balance, and ends in the standing pose. Played at 0.54 / `getUpDuration` speed so it ends when the body is upright.
